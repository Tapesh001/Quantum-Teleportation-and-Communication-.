What gets transferred?

Alice prepares a qubit. A shared Bell pair and two classical bits let Bob reconstruct its state. Alice’s displayed arrow is a saved reference for comparing the result.

What changes in our experiment?

Resource mixing p adds white noise to the Bell pair. The probability e applies to each classical bit. Independent errors can flip either bit separately. Linked errors flip both or neither.

How do we score it?

Fidelity measures overlap with the pure input. The balanced mean tests the positive and negative states of the X, Y and Z axes. The classical measure-and-prepare benchmark is 2/3 for this ensemble. All four measurement branches are included. Gates and measurements are ideal.
